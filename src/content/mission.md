## Independent businesses should be able to endure

Owner-run businesses are personal. The owner knows the customers, stands behind the work, and carries the consequences of every decision.

We want more of those businesses to last, create opportunity, and remain independent for the next generation.

## Support should not require a bigger company

Reliable back-office support has usually required another full-time hire, more management, or more software for the owner to maintain.

We believe small business owners should have access to dependable support without giving up control or changing the character of their business.

## Technology should strengthen ownership

Remi exists to give owners more control over their time, money, and commitments. The goal is not to run the business for them. It is to help more good businesses stay independent and grow on their own terms.

Remi is an AI teammate. We say that plainly because Remi will never pretend otherwise, and neither will we.
